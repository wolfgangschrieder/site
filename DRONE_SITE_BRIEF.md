# Drone retailer website — revised brief

NEXUS was an exploratory placeholder. The actual business is a small company selling drones.

## Goals
- Present civilian drone products professionally.
- Make product discovery and quote requests easy.
- Establish trust with verifiable company details only.

## Planned sections
Hero, catalog, applications (aerial photography, surveying, inspections, agriculture), company, consultation, contacts.

## Required inputs
Company name, product brands/models, target customers, geographic service area, pricing policy, contact methods.

## Design direction
Premium industrial technology, graphite backgrounds, crisp product imagery, restrained motion, mobile-first.

## Important
Do not invent stock, prices, certifications, warranty, testimonials or company history. Existing NEXUS implementation remains a prototype and must be replaced after product positioning is confirmed.
